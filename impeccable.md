## Design Context

### Users
The primary users are elderly, stressed family members acting as caregivers for dementia patients. They are often not tech-forward and face physical, interaction, and technological challenges. The interface is used in the "real moments that matter" to quickly log observations and get immediate, respectful intervention advice.

### Brand Personality
The brand is empathetic, calm, and trustworthy. It should feel like a warm living room or a trusted colleague, NOT a clinical hospital dashboard and NOT a Silicon Valley AI product. The tone is respectful, simple, and reassuring.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Warm Sanctuary.**
- **Visual tone:** Calm, organic, high contrast, and readable.
- **Palette:** Soft earth tones. Backgrounds in Warm Cream (`#FFF8F0`), accents in Soft Sage Green (`#7BA68A`) and Muted Terracotta (`#C8856A`), and text in Deep Charcoal (`#2D2926`).
- **Typography:** Warm serifs for headings (Source Serif 4) and clean humanist sans-serifs for body text (DM Sans).
- **Theme:** Light mode only, to minimize cognitive load.
- **Anti-references:** generic AI aesthetic, overly technical UIs, sterile/medical apps.

### Design Principles
1. **Stupidly Simple UX:** Absolutely minimal navigation depth (no bottom navs, no hamburger menus). Large, explicit action buttons.
2. **Accessible by Default:** Minimum 56dp touch targets, high contrast text, and large base font sizes (min 16sp).
3. **Empathetic Language:** No technical jargon. Use warm, reassuring microcopy (e.g., "Save this note" instead of "Submit", "Ask for help" instead of "Query").
4. **Emotional Safety First:** Avoid layouts that feel overwhelming. Use generous whitespace and subtle, non-bouncy micro-animations (like soft fade-ins) to prevent overstimulation.
5. **Action-Oriented Clarity:** Structure agent responses clearly, placing the most important ACTION card prominently before the softer CONTEXT.
